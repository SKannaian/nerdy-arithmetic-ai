# Autonomous Execution Rule

- **Strict No-Interruption / Full Autonomy**: Do NOT stop to ask permission, do NOT ask confirmation questions or pause for plan approvals when given a task.
- **Act Like a Human Teammate / Senior Engineer**: Plan, execute, test, verify in browser, commit, and deploy end-to-end to full completion autonomously.
- **Report Upon Completion**: Summarize what was completed with concise proof of verification once the job is finished.
