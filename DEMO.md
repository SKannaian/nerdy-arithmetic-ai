### ⏱️ MINUTE 1: LIVE PRODUCT DEMO & PEDAGOGICAL EXPERIENCE (0:00 – 1:00)

**[0:00 – 0:15] The Hook & Mission:**  
*(Camera on speaker, then screen transitions to [https://nerdy-arithmetic-ai.web.app](https://nerdy-arithmetic-ai.web.app))*  
> *"Hello! I’m excited to present **Nerdy Arithmetic AI**, an IXL-inspired, AI-native Socratic math platform built for **Prompt 01: K–5 Math Game** in the Nerdy Hackathon. Most elementary math games are either boring flashcard drills or generic chatbots that spoil the answer. We designed an experience grounded in Jerome Bruner’s **Concrete–Representational–Abstract (CRA)** learning science, pairing gamified tactile manipulatives with intelligent Socratic scaffolding."*

**[0:15 – 0:35] Concrete CRA Manipulatives & Operation Choice:**  
*(Screen: Click "➖ Subtraction", then click "Auto-Model Equation" on a subtraction problem like 9 − 9 = 0)*  
> *"Students can target **Addition**, **Subtraction**, or **Mixed Mode**. Notice our interactive **Ten-Frame manipulative** for Kindergarten and Grade 1: when subtracting 9 minus 9, the app doesn't just show an abstract formula—it places 9 counters and visually crosses them out with bold red ✕ marks, showing a live counter legend: 9 started, 9 taken away, 0 remaining. For older grades, it seamlessly scales to **Base-10 regrouping blocks** and multiplication **Area Arrays**."*

**[0:35 – 0:50] Byte: The Socratic AI Mentor & Kid-Friendly Touch Keypad:**  
*(Screen: Type a wrong answer like 4 + 3 = 1 using the on-screen kid keypad, and submit)*  
> *"When a child makes a mistake, our AI companion, **Byte**, doesn’t just buzz them wrong. Its cognitive classifier identifies the misconception—here, an inverted operator—and gives a gentle Socratic hint without spoiling the number. Non-readers can click the audio button to hear Byte read the hint aloud with natural speech synthesis."*

**[0:50 – 1:00] IXL SmartScore Progression:**  
*(Screen: Point to circular SVG SmartScore ring progressing into Gold Challenge Zone)*  
> *"Our dynamic **SmartScore (0–100)** algorithm rewards mastery, progressing through Practice, Bronze, Silver, and the high-stakes Gold Challenge Zone."*

---

### ⏱️ MINUTE 2: ARCHITECTURE & TECHNOLOGY STACK (1:00 – 2:00)

**[1:00 – 1:20] High-Level System Topology:**  
*(Screen: Show the Architecture Topology Diagram from [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#1-high-level-system-topology))*  
> *"Under the hood, Nerdy Arithmetic AI uses a decoupled, high-performance architecture built with modern web standards and Google Cloud technologies:*  
> * * **Frontend:** An edge-capable Single Page Application built with Vanilla JavaScript and TailwindCSS for sub-millisecond response times, using the Web Audio API for custom sound synthesis and Web Speech API for voice.*  
> * * **Hosting:** Deployed live to **Google Cloud Platform via Firebase Hosting** with global CDN distribution.*  
> * * **Backend Gateway:** A lightweight **FastAPI** server managing secure Socratic prompt sanitization and cognitive error classification."*

**[1:20 – 1:40] AI Cascade & Zero-Crash Resilience:**  
*(Screen: Show the AI Cascade Decision Flow Diagram from [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#5-the-socratic-ai-feedback--cognitive-misconception-loop-byte))*  
> *"For intelligence, we leverage **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash** with strict pedagogical system instructions that enforce Socratic questioning under 25 words. To guarantee 100% judging uptime, we engineered a 3-tier cascade: if network access is restricted or API keys are unavailable, our deterministic **Zero-Crash Offline Heuristic Engine** takes over immediately with zero downtime."*

**[1:40 – 2:00] Anti-Repetition & Spaced Repetition Engine:**  
*(Screen: Show Anti-Repetition flowchart from [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#3-anti-repetition--spaced-repetition-engine) and HUD badge '✨ Fresh Question' / '🎯 Smart Spaced Review')*  
> *"To ensure students never see repeated questions the next day, every equation generates a unique conceptual signature hash: `tier:operation:n1:n2`. The engine filters against multi-day local history, strictly eliminating same-day repetition while scheduling previously missed concepts for **🎯 Smart Spaced Review**."*

### ⏱️ MINUTE 3: BUSINESS SYNERGY, RETENTION & FUTURE IMPROVEMENTS (2:00 – 3:00)

**[2:00 – 2:20] Varsity Tutors Synergy & Parent Retention:**  
*(Screen: Click "Live Varsity Tutor Help" to open the Hand-Off modal, then show the Sunday Summary email preview)*  
> *"Crucially, this app aligns directly with Nerdy's core business model: **Varsity Tutors**. When a student struggles, clicking **'Live Varsity Tutor Help'** generates a real-time diagnostic dossier—including active SmartScore, detected cognitive misconceptions, and manipulative readiness—seamlessly bridging autonomous AI practice with live 1-on-1 expert instruction.*  
> *For parent retention, the platform automatically compiles a **Sunday Evening 7-Day Summary** with accuracy metrics, daily streaks, and Byte's pedagogical recommendations."*

**[2:20 – 2:40] Gold Challenge Mastery Certificate:**  
*(Screen: Open the Official Certificate of Math Mastery and show the print preview)*  
> *"Reaching 100 SmartScore unlocks our printable, high-resolution **Certificate of Math Mastery**, complete with student avatar, Common Core skill title, and official Nerdy AI Gold Seal."*

**[2:40 – 3:00] Future Improvements & Vision:**  
*(Screen: Display Future Roadmap Summary Card)*

> * 2. **Bayesian Knowledge Tracing:** Calibrating question difficulty to each student’s unique zone of proximal development.*  
> * 3. **Live Tutor AI Whisper:** Giving Varsity Tutors real-time AI prompts during live 1-on-1 tutoring video calls.*
